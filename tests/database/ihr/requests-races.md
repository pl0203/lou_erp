2. Session B starts BEGIN and invokes the same RPC with command_b/payload_b. For last_allowance and same_date require SQLSTATE 55000; for same_key require success with the identical original A receipt. B cannot complete before A releases.
3. The owner polls pg_stat_activity for the recorded B PID until wait_event_type='Lock' AND wait_event='advisory'. 4. Only after observing that barrier, send COMMIT to A. Then require B's specified outcome and commit its assertion transaction. Both actual-role assertion streams must complete cleanly.
